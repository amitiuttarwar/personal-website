About Me

Hi, meet Amiti.

Amiti Uttarwar is the first female contributor to Bitcoin Core. Her protocol improvements on the peer-to-peer layer to improve security and privacy landed her on Forbes 30 under 30. She is passionate about Bitcoin’s potential to redefine our societal ideas of wealth due to its global and inclusive nature.

Amiti is currently focused on building Waye, an organization that promotes psycho-social support for open source developers.

Her other interests include off-trail backpacking, dancing, and meditation.
